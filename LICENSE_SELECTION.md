# License Selection Notes — Decision Required

This file is guidance, not a license grant. Do not publish a license until the maintainers choose one and include the complete official license text.

## Application/source code
- **Apache-2.0:** permissive reuse, includes express patent license and NOTICE mechanism.
- **AGPL-3.0:** copyleft; modified network-served versions generally have source-sharing obligations under its terms.
- **MIT:** simple permissive license, but less explicit patent language than Apache-2.0.

Choose based on the project's adoption, contribution and sustainability goals. Obtain legal advice if the choice has material commercial implications.

## Educational content
Consider a separate content license such as **CC BY 4.0** for original written experiment material, diagrams and learning resources, if attribution-based reuse is desired. Do not apply a content license to software code by accident.

## Third-party material
- Record source, author, license, version and required attribution.
- Verify that redistribution and modification are allowed.
- Do not copy text, diagrams, images, videos or simulations merely because they are publicly accessible.
- Review each asset and dependency independently.
- Maintain `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

- [ ] Application license selected and full text added.
- [ ] Content license selected and stated clearly.
- [ ] Contributor terms and ownership expectations reviewed.
- [ ] All third-party assets checked.
