# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful and constructive community.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful, patient and constructive.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Welcome different levels of experience and backgrounds.
- Give actionable feedback and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, deliberate disruption, doxxing, sexual harassment, and abusive or demeaning conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting
Report concerns privately to the project maintainers using the contact channel published in the repository. Do not include sensitive personal data in public issues. Maintainers should respond fairly, confidentially where possible, and proportionately.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or remove access for serious or repeated violations.
