# Product Vision

## Vision
Create an accessible, open-source virtual chemistry laboratory that helps school and college students understand chemistry through structured learning and scientifically responsible interactive experiments.

## Problem
Students may have limited access to laboratory equipment, time, supervision or repeatable practice opportunities. Static notes and videos can explain procedures but often cannot provide safe, repeatable interaction with variables and measurements.

## Proposed value
- Explore chemistry concepts through interactive experiments.
- Understand equipment, procedures, equations and observations.
- Change supported variables and see modelled outcomes.
- Record observations and calculations in a practical notebook.
- Learn from transparent explanations, assumptions and limitations.
- Access a consistent experience across web and mobile over time.

## Audience
- School learners, with age-appropriate content and safeguarding.
- Undergraduate learners.
- Teachers and laboratory instructors.
- Educators and subject-matter contributors.

## Product principles
1. Accuracy over spectacle.
2. Explain the model, not just the animation.
3. Never disguise simulated data as real measurements.
4. Design for accessibility and low-bandwidth environments.
5. Support curriculum mapping without locking content to one board.
6. Make content review and provenance visible.
7. Prefer small, complete learning experiences over a large shallow catalogue.

## Success indicators
- Learners can complete an experiment and explain its scientific principle.
- Model outputs match reviewed reference cases within stated tolerances.
- Educators can review and contribute structured content.
- Core learning flows work on supported devices and assistive technologies.
- Users can distinguish simulation from physical laboratory practice.

## Non-goals for the initial release
- Replacing physical laboratories or safety training.
- Simulating every chemical system with research-grade fidelity.
- Providing unrestricted instructions for hazardous synthesis or misuse.
- Building social networking, payments, or an AI tutor before core learning quality is proven.
