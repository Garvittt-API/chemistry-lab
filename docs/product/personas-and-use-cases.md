# Personas and Use Cases

These are provisional design personas. Validate them with actual students and educators before treating them as research findings.

## School learner
Needs clear language, guided steps, safe examples, visual feedback and short learning sessions. Avoid assuming prior laboratory experience.

## Undergraduate learner
Needs equations, units, calculations, measurement concepts, error analysis and deeper explanations.

## Teacher / laboratory instructor
Needs curriculum mapping, learning objectives, reviewable procedures, class-friendly access and confidence that models are transparent.

## Content contributor
Needs a clear authoring schema, references, review checklist, licensing guidance and a way to propose corrections without being a software engineer.

## Core use cases
1. Find an experiment matching a topic and level.
2. Read theory and safety context before starting.
3. Select permitted inputs and operate a simulation.
4. Observe modelled output and understand why it changes.
5. Record observations and complete calculations.
6. Answer reflection questions and review misconceptions.
7. Export a practical record when that capability is implemented.
8. Report a scientific or accessibility issue.
