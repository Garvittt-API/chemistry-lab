# Design Principles

## Experience goals
The product should feel like a trustworthy learning instrument: clear, calm, precise and engaging. Visual effects must never obscure measurements, labels, hazards or scientific explanations.

## Principles
- **Clarity:** one primary task per screen; use plain language.
- **Scientific legibility:** units, significant figures, scales and uncertainty remain visible.
- **Progressive disclosure:** explain fundamentals first; reveal advanced detail when useful.
- **Direct manipulation:** controls should map clearly to physical variables.
- **Feedback:** show what changed, why it changed and whether the input is valid.
- **Consistency:** use common patterns for apparatus, measurement, reset and observation.
- **Accessibility:** never rely on color, sound, motion or drag gestures alone.
- **Trust:** label simulated, theoretical and user-entered data distinctly.

## Visual direction
Use a modern scientific/editorial interface rather than a game dashboard. A restrained palette may use neutral surfaces with one primary accent and clear semantic colors. Avoid neon overload, excessive glassmorphism, tiny monospace text and decorative animations.

## Layout
- Desktop: content and experiment workspace can sit side-by-side where space permits.
- Mobile: prioritize one-column guided flow; keep controls reachable and avoid precision drag interactions.
- Experiment canvas: reserve stable space for apparatus/visualization and keep controls and readings outside the canvas where possible.

## Motion
Motion should communicate state changes, not decorate. Respect reduced-motion settings. Provide pause/reset and avoid flashing effects.

## Phase 02 visual direction

The web foundation uses a scientific editorial composition: strong reading widths, rule-based
separation, restrained elevation and one measured primary accent. Demonstration content is visually
distinct from reviewed content through a text badge and status language. Cards are used where they
support discovery; long-form experiment information uses sections, tables and a persistent index
instead of an undifferentiated card grid.
