# Experiment Authoring and Review Workflow

## Workflow
1. Proposal: contributor submits topic, audience, learning outcomes and references.
2. Draft: author fills the experiment specification.
3. Technical review: schema, links, assets, units and model interface checked.
4. Scientific review: equations, assumptions, reference cases and interpretation checked by a qualified reviewer.
5. Safety review: audience, hazards, exposure routes and institutional context assessed.
6. Accessibility review: controls, diagrams and explanations have accessible alternatives.
7. Approval: maintainer records reviewer, scope, date and version.
8. Release: content is versioned and changelog updated.
9. Maintenance: corrections and deprecation are tracked.

## Review independence
Where feasible, the author should not be the sole scientific approver. AI-generated content is always a draft until reviewed by a qualified human.

## Corrections
Provide a visible route to report scientific errors. Critical safety or scientific defects should trigger prompt withdrawal or warning while investigated.
